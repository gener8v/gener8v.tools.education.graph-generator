## Findings

### QR-001: The overlap property never generates wrapped text, and its oracle misfires when it does

**Category:** Testing
**Severity:** Concern
**Location:** `tests/layout/circle.test.ts:201-243` (string arbitraries at `:202` and `:212`; oracle `boxOf` at `:30-35` and `overlaps` at `:52-61`)
**Description:** This property is the figure's evidence for GSS-NFR-003: no label overlaps another label, a segment or the outline. It is also the only test that combines titles, units and custom text. Two problems stack.

1. **The strings are never long.** `fc.string({ maxLength: 120 })` and `fc.string({ maxLength: 80 })` use fast-check's default `size: "small"`. That caps generated length at 10 characters, whatever `maxLength` says. Sampling each arbitrary 3,000 times gave a maximum length of 10. So in 300 runs no label wraps, no title wraps, and two labels are never stacked below the figure (DEL-006).
2. **The oracle reports false overlaps once strings are long.** With `size: "max"`, the property fails on unmodified code.
   - Shrunk counterexample: `title: "%0 !@@@@<@ @M<00@ MM@CMmG"`, `unit: "cm"`, `radiusLabelText: ""`, `diameterLabelText: "r"`, `circumferenceLabelText: ""`.
   - The two title lines touch exactly, at y 16 → 37.6 and 37.6 → 59.2.
   - `boxOf` rebuilds each line's box from its rounded `y`. Floating-point error then gives 37.599999999999994 against 37.60000000000001, so `placeLabel` counts the touching lines as overlapping.
   - The layout is not at fault. With each rebuilt box shrunk by 0.01 (the `fmt()` rounding step), 3 × 2,000 runs with `size: "max"` pass, with three different seeds.

**Mutation evidence** (on a copy outside the repository). Two mutations pass the whole suite, and the corrected property catches both:
- setting `textLines`' line spacing to the font size, so wrapped lines overlap by 2.8 units;
- letting label candidates rise into the title band (`p.y >= 0` in `inside`, `src/layout/circle.ts:167-168`).
**Impact:** The test reads as covering 120-character labels and 80-character titles, but it only checks short, single-line text. Wrapping and stacking are where overlaps are most likely, and they are the paths later figures inherit. Anyone who raises the size without also fixing the oracle will get a misleading red test.
**Recommendation:**
- Generate long text with `fc.string({ maxLength: 120, size: "max" })`, and the same for the title.
- In `boxOf`, shrink each rebuilt box by 0.01 on every side, or merge the lines of one text block into a single box before checking.
- `numRuns: 300` can stay: the corrected property ran 2,000 runs in about a second.
**Status:** Resolved
**Resolution:** Fixed (approved by Thomas, 2026-10-13). The label and title arbitraries use `size: "max"`, and the overlap oracle uses `reservedBoxOf`, each rebuilt box shrunk by `fmt()`'s 0.01 step. It passed eight runs with fresh seeds. Moving label candidates into the title band, or setting the line spacing to the font size, now fails it.

### QR-002: The shared title block and `sourceOf` have no tests of their own

**Category:** Testing
**Severity:** Concern
**Location:** `src/layout/kit/title.ts` (`textLines` `:18-40`, `titleBlock` `:47-56`); `src/core/format.ts:186-193` (`sourceOf`). Expected tests: `tests/layout/kit/title.test.ts`, `tests/core/format.test.ts`
**Description:** Every other module in `src/layout/kit/` has its own test file in `tests/layout/kit/`: `check`, `format`, `labels`, `scale` and `text`.
- `title.ts` is new, and DEL-004 makes it the title and multi-line-text helper for every later layout. It is reached only through the circle's Scenes. Two circle tests touch it: the title is drawn above the circle, and a blank title draws nothing.
- `sourceOf` is new in `src/core/format.ts`. That module has `tests/core/format.test.ts`, but no test names `sourceOf`.

These mutations on a copy each pass all 304 tests of the affected files:
- the title is no longer bold;
- the title wraps to the full 400-unit width, losing its side margins;
- `titleBlock`'s height leaves out its top margin;
- multi-line spacing is set to the font size, so lines overlap;
- `sourceOf` treats a value missing from the provenance as supplied. That inverts its documented rule that such a value is "never shown with digits nobody wrote".

Only moving the baseline is caught, and only by the circle's overlap property.
**Impact:** The square, triangle, cube, cylinder and the graph types will draw titles and wrapped labels through `textLines` and `titleBlock`, and read values' sources through `sourceOf`. A regression in either would show up, if at all, as an unexplained difference in some figure's Scene, not as a failing kit test that names the helper.
**Recommendation:**
- Add `tests/layout/kit/title.test.ts`:
  - no title, or one that is blank after cleaning, gives `{ nodes: [], height: 0 }`;
  - a long title wraps within `SCENE_WIDTH - 2 * SCENE_MARGIN`, with every line centered at 200 and bold;
  - consecutive lines are `LINE_HEIGHT × fontSize` apart;
  - `height` is `SCENE_MARGIN` plus lines × line height.
- Add `sourceOf` cases to `tests/core/format.test.ts`:
  - supplied;
  - derived;
  - missing (→ derived);
  - an inherited key such as `toString` (→ derived).
**Status:** Resolved
**Resolution:** Fixed (approved by Thomas, 2026-10-13).
- New `tests/layout/kit/title.test.ts` covers `textLines` (one node per line, centered, font, color, weight, line spacing) and `titleBlock` (blank → nothing, one bold centered line, wrapping within the side margins, height).
- `sourceOf` cases added to `tests/core/format.test.ts`: supplied, derived, missing, inherited.
- All five mutations from the finding now fail.

### QR-004: Figure-agnostic label placement is private to the circle layout, and the next figure is told to follow it

**Category:** DRY
**Severity:** Concern
**Location:** `src/layout/circle.ts`:
- `:18-20`: `GAP`, `LABEL_MAX_WIDTH`;
- `:28-32`: `measure`;
- `:54-65`: `besideHorizontal`, `besideVertical`;
- `:67-78`: `line`;
- `:166-198`: the bounds filter, the place-or-stack loop and the DEL-006 fallback.
**Description:** About 60 of the layout's 206 lines have nothing to do with circles:
- sizing wrapped label text;
- candidate positions beside a horizontal or a vertical segment;
- a stroked segment node;
- the keep-inside-the-drawing filter;
- the loop that places each label with `placeLabel`, adds it as an obstacle (AD-010), and stacks the ones it cannot place below the figure (DEL-006).

They are module-private, and three documents point the next figure at them:
- GSS TICKET-003's Prior Art says to read `src/layout/circle.ts` and "follow the same per-type pattern".
- Its Known Hazard says unplaced labels are stacked below the figure (DEL-006).
- The delivery record's Notes say "the square and other types copy this layout".

DEL-004 moved the title into the kit so that no type ticket would copy it. The same reasoning applies here, to more code that holds more rules. `circleLayout` itself is 116 lines (`:91-206`) mixing geometry, obstacles, candidates, placement and fallback. Moving the generic part out would leave it about the circle's geometry.

This finding is about where the fallback's code lives, not about its behaviour, which CR-004 / DEL-006 settled.
**Impact:** With four more figures planned in this area, each copy repeats the AD-010 obstacle rule, the bounds filter and the DEL-006 stack. A later fix to any of them would have to be made, and tested, in every layout. One example is keeping a stacked label next to its measurement name, which CR-004 considered.
**Recommendation:** Before GSS TICKET-003 starts, move the generic pieces into the kit:
- `labelSize(text, fontSize, maxWidth)`;
- `besideHorizontal` and `besideVertical` as candidate generators, next to `placeLabel` in `src/layout/kit/labels.ts`;
- a `segmentNode(segment, color)`;
- `placeLabels(requests, obstacles, bounds, stackTop)`, returning the nodes and the new bottom;
- `GAP` and `LABEL_MAX_WIDTH` as kit constants.

The circle then keeps only its geometry and its choice of candidates. If this is deferred instead, append a Known Hazard to GSS TICKET-003 asking it to extract these pieces when the square needs them, not copy them.
**Status:** Deferred → TICKET-003
**Resolution:** Deferred → TICKET-003 (approved by Thomas, 2026-10-13). A Known Hazard is appended to GSS TICKET-003: extract the figure-agnostic label code into the layout kit when the square needs it, shaped by both figures, with kit tests, and switch the circle to it. Do not copy it.

### QR-005: The registry entries are cast past the type checker, and the table form checks nothing anyway

**Category:** Patterns
**Severity:** Suggestion
**Location:** `src/core/definitions/index.ts:19` (`circle as unknown as GraphTypeDefinition`); `src/layout/registry.ts:32` (`circleLayout as Layout`); `src/react/createGraphComponent.tsx:109` (comment)
**Description:**
- **Both casts are unnecessary.** I type-checked the same two table forms with no cast, in a scratch file outside the repository, using the repository's TypeScript in strict mode: exit 0. That is what the bivariant method syntax in `src/core/definitions/types.ts` and `src/layout/registry.ts`, and the type-level test at `tests/core/definitions.test.ts:225`, were designed to allow.
- **The table form checks nothing about its entries anyway.** `Object.assign(target as Partial<Record<…>>, { … })` types the result as an intersection, so `{ circle: { type: "circle" } }`, with no `describe`, `properties` or `checks`, compiles with or without the cast.
- **`satisfies` restores the check.** Adding `satisfies Partial<Record<GraphType, GraphTypeDefinition>>` (or `Partial<Record<GraphType, Layout>>`) to the object literal:
  - rejects the broken entry with TS2740;
  - accepts the real `circle` and `circleLayout` with no cast;
  - leaves the `/* @__PURE__ */` marks, and the source pattern the bundle test matches, unchanged.
- **A comment is now wrong.** `src/react/createGraphComponent.tsx:109` still calls the factory "the one place a type's own definition and layout widen to the shared table types".
**Impact:** Low today, because runtime tests catch the likely mistakes: `tests/core/definitions.test.ts:214` checks keys against types, and the definition-rules tests run over every registered definition. But every type ticket adds one line to each table (`index.ts`'s own doc), so the double cast would be copied ten more times. A layout registered under the wrong type would be caught only at runtime, if at all.
**Recommendation:** Drop both casts and add `satisfies` to each table's object literal. Update the comment at `src/react/createGraphComponent.tsx:109`.
**Status:** Resolved
**Resolution:** Fixed (approved by Thomas, 2026-10-13).
- Both table literals end in `satisfies Partial<Record<GraphType, …>>`, and both casts are gone.
- The factory's two casts were also unnecessary and are removed; its comment now says why none is needed.
- An incomplete entry fails with TS2740, checked on a scratch edit and then restored.
- The bundle test still passes.

### QR-007: The snapping that makes `besideOutline` choose its centered branches is unexplained

**Category:** Readability
**Severity:** Suggestion
**Location:** `src/layout/circle.ts:43-44`
**Description:** `Math.round(Math.cos(angle) * 1e9) / 1e9`, and the same for `sin`, is load-bearing:
- `Math.cos(π/2)` is 6.1e-17, and `Math.sin(π)` is 1.2e-16.
- Without the snap, the sign tests on lines 47-48 would treat the top candidate as right of center and the left candidate as above center. Those boxes would then be placed off-center.

No comment says this, and the function's doc describes the candidates by angle only. Removing the snap passes every test, including the corrected overlap property from QR-001: the circumference placement test checks only the 45° candidate.
**Impact:** A reader, or a later figure that copies the function, could take the rounding for noise and remove it, and nothing would fail.
**Recommendation:** Replace the angle list with an explicit table of unit directions (`[Math.SQRT1_2, Math.SQRT1_2]`, `[0, 1]`, `[1, 0]`, …) so the zero components are exact. Or keep the snap and add a one-line comment saying why it is there.
**Status:** Resolved
**Resolution:** Fixed (approved by Thomas, 2026-10-13). `besideOutline` reads the exact unit vectors in `OUTLINE_DIRECTIONS`; there are no angles and no rounding. It is exported and tested directly: every candidate lies outside the circle, and the four axis candidates are centered. A direction of `Math.cos(π / 2)` instead of 0 fails that test.
