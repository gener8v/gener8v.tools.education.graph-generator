Added: the `cylinder` type, as `<CylinderGraph>` and the `./cylinder` entry. An upright cylinder is drawn to scale from its radius or diameter and its height, in the same isometric view as the other solids. The hidden half of the base is dashed or left out. You can draw and label the radius or diameter on the top face, and label the height beside the side, with the value and unit or your own text. The circle and the cylinder share the terms `radius`, `diameter`, `showRadius`, `showDiameter`, `labelRadius`, `labelDiameter`, `radiusLabelText` and `diameterLabelText`.
