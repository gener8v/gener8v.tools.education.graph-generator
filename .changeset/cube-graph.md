Added: the `cube` type, as `<CubeGraph>` and the `./cube` entry. A cube is drawn from its edge length in the same isometric view as the other solids, with all twelve edges the same drawn length. The three hidden edges are dashed or left out. You can label one edge, or the three visible edges that meet at the front corner, with the length and unit or your own text.
